MERCATOR — the house as a world

The kenshi.reborn engine draws the gates of the house as a world: two houses, the machines between them, the wires. It does not carry a list of who is alive. It asks the mesh, and lights each gate by how it stands there.

How to read it

  • Lit, in its own color: a body of the mesh that answers.
  • Dark red: a body of the mesh that did not answer.
  • Grey: a gate of the house that is not a body of the mesh.
  • Green arch: two bodies standing on the same commit.
  • Amber arch: two bodies that answer but stand on different commits.
  • Ring of small bodies: the primals a gate says it runs. Green is up, red is down.

A new picture

The picture is a still: it shows the mesh as it was when it was drawn, and the table beside it asks the mesh again each time this page opens. The engine draws a new one without a window, and writes the names beside it:
