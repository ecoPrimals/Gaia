The Operating System Runs from 201 Townsend

Dykema doesn’t pick sides. It grows branches for whoever pays.

In 1993, Dykema drafted Public Act 362 — the charter school law. In 1997, Dykema defended it at the Michigan Supreme Court. Today, Dykema represents authorizers, forms dark money vehicles, runs PAC compliance for both parties, and employs the woman who testified under oath to managing 80+ dark money accounts.
