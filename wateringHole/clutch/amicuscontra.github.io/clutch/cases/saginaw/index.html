Saginaw: The Ghost Witness

An attorney submitted a witness who does not exist. 13 convergence points confirm non-existence. IC3 complaint filed. Walk the evidence.

The Fabrication

In a contempt hearing, an attorney submitted an affidavit from a witness named “Samantha Aljouny.” When journalist Rita Williams investigated, she found 13 independent data points confirming this person does not exist — no Michigan address, no driver’s license, no voter registration, no employer, no social media presence older than 3 months.

When she published her findings, the court signed a contempt order against her.
