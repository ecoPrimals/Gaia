About Clutch Justice

Independent investigative journalism covering Michigan court accountability, charter school fraud, and institutional capture.

What This Is

Clutch Justice is an independent investigative journalism platform covering Michigan’s courts, schools, and oversight systems.

This site — clutch.primals.eco — is the collaborative investigation layer. It takes the evidence published across the primals.eco network and makes it walkable. Patrons and visitors can navigate the investigation graph, explore connections, verify evidence, and help identify patterns that strengthen active cases.

The cycle is the design.

Who We Are

People who read court records, file FOIA requests, attend hearings, submit complaints, and publish what we find.

We are not attorneys. We are not parties to each other’s cases. We are citizens exercising First Amendment rights to document institutional accountability — or its absence.

Some of us are investigative journalists. Some are parents. Some are both.

Principles

  1. Every fact is sourced. No claim without a citation. No finding without a document.
  2. Every correction is published. If we get something wrong, we fix it publicly.
  3. No tracking. No cookies. No identifying data. No surveillance of visitors.
  4. Cross-protection by design. Evidence exists across multiple nodes. Suppress one — the others hold.
  5. Open source. Clone the repositories. Verify everything. Trust the documents, not us.

The Name

Clutch carries two frequencies:

The Handbag

The thing you grab when you’re going somewhere important. Everything you need, nothing you don’t. The investigation evidence, held tight, carried with purpose. Rita’s frequency.

The Transmission

The mechanical clutch — the mechanism that engages and disengages power. The engine (journalism) generates force. The drivetrain (evidence network) delivers it. The clutch is the engagement mechanism that decides when and how power transfers from investigation to accountability.

Press the clutch — disengage. Gather evidence. Build the case. Walk the graph.

Release the clutch — engage. File the complaints. Publish the findings. Hold the institutions accountable.

The handbag and the transmission are the same object. Form and function. Both clutch.
