How to Contribute

Every visitor is an investigator. Here's how to help walk the case, verify evidence, and flag connections that strengthen the network.

Every Visitor Is an Investigator

You don’t need a law degree. You don’t need journalist credentials. You need the ability to read public records and follow documented evidence from one node to the next.

What You Can Do Right Now

🔍 Walk the Graph

Open the investigation graph and explore. Start with any node. Follow the edges. If you find a path between two nodes that isn’t documented — that’s a finding.

📋 Pull Public Records

Every actor page has an Investigation Path section with unchecked items. These are research targets nobody has hit yet. Pick one and run it:

📝 File FOIA Requests

Michigan’s Freedom of Information Act gives you the right to request public records from any government agency. Our FOIA Toolkit has templates ready to use.

⚠️ Flag Connections

See two nodes that should be connected? A person who appears in multiple investigations? A public record that contradicts what’s on the graph?

Email: hello@clutchjustice.com

✅ Verify Existing Evidence

Don’t trust us. Trust the sources. Every claim on every evidence site can be independently verified:

  • Clone the git repository and audit it
  • Check every LARA ID, every case number, every campaign finance filing
  • If something is wrong, tell us — truth matters more than being right

Sourcing Standard

We don’t accept tips without sources. Every claim must be tied to:

Source TypeWhat Counts
Court recordsCase number, court, date, docket entry
Public filingsLARA entity ID, campaign finance filing, ARIN WHOIS
FOIA responsesRequesting party, responding agency, date, tracking number
Published reportingOutlet, author, date, URL
StatutesMCL or MCR citation

If you can’t source it, put it in an investigation checklist as a - [ ] item — a lead to pursue, not a finding.

What We Will Never Ask For

  • Your name (unless you choose to share it)
  • Your location
  • Your relationship to any case
  • Money
  • Access to private systems or databases

We work with public records because public records are permanent, verifiable, and belong to everyone.

The Network Protects You

This platform is part of a cross-protection evidence network. Evidence is published across multiple nodes (detroit.primals.eco, barry.primals.eco, tuebor.primals.eco). Contributions verified and added to the graph become part of that network.

SLAPP one node — the others amplify. This is by design.
