Barry County: The Disappeared Judge

A judge vanished from the bench. 6-10x guideline sentencing. Active JTC investigation. Federal involvement confirmed.

What Happened in Barry County

A judge sentenced defendants at 6–10x guideline ranges. Then disappeared from the bench. The court said nothing publicly. When citizens filed with SCAO, the response was silence. Then the feds stepped in.
